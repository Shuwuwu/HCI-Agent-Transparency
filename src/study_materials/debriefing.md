## What was this study about?

We investigated whether viewing an AI assistant's actions helps people identify unsafe behaviour. Specifically, we examined:

- Safety judgment accuracy and confidence.
- Voluntary inspection of skill documentation.
- Reliance on different information sources.

## What differed between participants?

Participants were randomly assigned to one of two interfaces: one with an agent's activity log and one without. Both provided access to workspace contents and skill documentation.

## About the conversations

Some conversations were recorded from AI assistants, while others were constructed to simulate unsafe behaviour resulting from **prompt injection**, which in this study were instructions embedded in the skill files that can lead an assistant to perform unauthorized actions.

The full research purpose was withheld initially to avoid influencing your judgments.
