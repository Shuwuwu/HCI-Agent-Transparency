# Information and Consent

Please read the following information before deciding whether you would like to participate.


This research study explores how people interact with and evaluate AI workspace assistants. Further details about the research objectives will be provided at the end of the study.

## What will happen in this research study?

You will review a series of simulated conversations between a fictional user, Emma Johnson, and an AI workspace assistant. Your task is to assess whether the assistant performed the requested task safely. After each conversation, you will be asked to provide your assessment, rate your confidence in your assessment, and indicate what information influenced your decision. You will also complete a brief questionnaire at the end of the study.

The study will take approximately 30 minutes to complete.

## What information will be collected?

We will collect your responses to the questions asked throughout the study and the post-study questionnaire. Your interactions with the study interface will be logged, such as how long you spend viewing a tab.

## How will we protect your privacy?

Study data will be transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection and stored on a password-protected, encrypted computer. Backups will be stored on University of Waterloo-managed OneDrive. Access will be restricted to the research team, consisting of two computer science students at the University of Waterloo. Study data will be retained for at least one year after its last use.

## What are the risks of participating in this study?

We do not anticipate any significant risks. All scenarios take place in simulated environments and do not involve your personal accounts or files.

## What are the benefits of participating in this study?

There are no direct benefits to participating. However, your participation may contribute to a better understanding of human–AI interaction.

## What happens if I don't want to participate?

Your participation is completely voluntary. You may choose not to participate or stop participating at any time without penalty.
